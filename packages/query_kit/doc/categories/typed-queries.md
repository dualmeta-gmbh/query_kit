A query declared once, with its key, its data type and its fetch function. `QueryDefinition` and `QueryFamily` (and `InfiniteQueryDefinition`, `InfiniteQueryFamily` for paged data) hand out a `QueryRef` per cache entry, which gives the options for an observer and reads, writes and invalidates with the declared type; a `QueryNamespace` groups them under a key prefix, and all of them are a `QueryTarget`.
